FAQ

Check-in is from 14:00. Check-out is until 11:00. We have a self check-in system, so you can arrive at any time after 14:00 without needing to meet anyone.
You can book directly through our website or contact us via WhatsApp or Telegram. We accept card payments. You can also call us at +380 95 588 82 22.
Please contact us directly for cancellation terms — they may vary depending on the booking dates and apartment type. We always try to find the best solution for our guests.
The minimum stay is 1 night. We also welcome long-term guests — please contact us for weekly or monthly rates.
Please contact us in advance to discuss pet-friendly options. We consider each case individually and do our best to accommodate our guests.
Unfortunately, the elevator in the building is currently out of order. Please keep this in mind when booking, especially if you have heavy luggage.
